The user requested real email/call delivery to an address or number entered on the manual demo cards, rather than limiting every manual test to one server-pinned contact. The separate deep UI/UX audit is deferred; a teammate is editing `app/globals.css`, which was left untouched.

The professor demo password accepts any nonempty value and **is not identity verification**. To avoid turning that public login into an arbitrary-recipient mailer/dialer, manual live delivery to an entered contact is separately gated: `DEMO_LIVE_AUTOMATIONS=true` **and** `DEMO_LIVE_MANUAL_RECIPIENTS=true` on the VPS, a JWT session freshly established by the allowlisted Google `google-professor` OAuth account, and explicit confirmation that the recipient agreed to this test. `hasCalendar` alone is insufficient; a demo-password session can retain a previously connected Calendar. The dashboard offers re-verification even if the Calendar was previously connected. A checkbox is only an operator attestation, **not independently verified consent**; the operator must obtain real recipient permission before testing. These safeguards do not turn the demo into a production consent-management system.

Without the extra opt-in, live test contacts remain server-pinned. Public mode now accepts any one syntactically valid entered email or Indian `+91` mobile number for **simulation only**; nobody is contacted. Import-triggered actions, weekly summary and adviser escalation remain pinned and unaffected by manual entered contacts. Only fixed synthetic 69% example content is sent; no imported student contact or academic information is sent. A Convex atomic claim limits manual email and manual call to ten attempts **each per mode and provider** per professor per UTC day and records only aggregate action state, not the entered destination. Failed/uncertain attempts consume an allowance. Provider acceptance is not confirmed inbox receipt or an answered call. Resend `onboarding@resend.dev` sandbox delivery is limited to the Resend owner; other addresses require a verified sending domain and compliant provider account.
