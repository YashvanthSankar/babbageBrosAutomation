A failed smoke check is a release blocker, not a documentation problem. 2. With **synthetic records only**, verify authenticated professor sign-in and upload/read flows, a student sign-in scoped to their own record, booking and local collision response, manual email simulation, weekly summary simulation, duplicate weekly `already_run`, and conditional adviser activity. The anonymous smoke check does not verify any of these. Do not upload real records or contact student addresses.

4. Rotate the previously exposed Google OAuth client secret. Configure the approved account on VPS, have the account owner explicitly grant Calendar consent, verify connection/FreeBusy and one **consented synthetic** booking on that primary calendar. Google writes may send student invitations; do not run this against unconsenting recipients.
5. For optional live email/voice checks, require explicit `DEMO_LIVE_AUTOMATIONS=true`, a consenting server-pinned Resend-owner inbox/test number, a verified sender/provider setup, and a real inbox/phone observation. Provider acceptance is not confirmed receipt. Keep public mode simulated otherwise.
6. Replace the impersonable demo login and remediate dependency advisories before any real student data or production identity claim. Monitor failed/pending claims; automatic retries are intentionally absent to avoid duplicate provider sends.

Dashboard risk projections and Convex automation/cohort queries independently calculate risk from the same stored records. The tested synthetic scenarios agree, but exhaustive parity across corrections, ties and boundary thresholds is not yet established; add differential regression cases before claiming exact equivalence.
